# User Guide / Tataiso ea Tšebeliso

## English

### About the app

Moshoeshoe II Learning Hub is an in-progress learning app with lessons in Sesotho and English. Lesson content is included in the app. Learner profiles and learning progress are stored on the current device.

### Start learning

1. Choose Sesotho or English.
2. Choose a grade and subject.
3. Open a lesson and read its sections. Use the read-aloud control when it is available on the device.
4. Open Practice or Revision to answer questions and review saved mistakes.
5. Open **Me** to view the selected learner's profile and recorded progress.

### Learner profiles and privacy

- Use a nickname, not a learner's full name, phone number, or other identifying information.
- When adding a learner, choose an avatar and grade, select a gender option, and confirm that a parent or guardian has agreed.
- Use **Change learner** when more than one learner shares the device.
- Profile and progress information stays on that device. It is not synchronized to a server.

### Settings and access

Use Settings to change the language and available display or reading options. Read-aloud depends on voices installed on the device.

### Current limitations

This prototype does not yet include the full Thuso chat helper, the requested games and interactive widgets, starting and final checks, learner feedback collection, or the complete PIN-protected teacher and M&E tools. An installed APK has not yet been verified in airplane mode.

## Sesotho / Sesotho

### Ka sesebelisoa sena

Moshoeshoe II Learning Hub ke sesebelisoa sa thuto se ntseng se ntlafatsoa. Se na le lithuto ka Sesotho le Senyesemane. Litaba tsa lithuto li kentsoe ka har'a sesebelisoa. Liprofaele le tsoelopele ea baithuti li bolokoa fonong ena feela.

### Qala ho ithuta

1. Khetha Sesotho kapa Senyesemane.
2. Khetha sehlopha le thuto.
3. Bula thuto 'me u bale likarolo tsa eona. Sebelisa konopo ea ho mamela ha e fumaneha fonong.
4. Bula **Boikoetliso** kapa **Pheta** ho araba lipotso le ho hlahloba liphoso tse bolokiloeng.
5. Bula **Nna** ho bona profaele ea moithuti ea khethiloeng le tsoelopele e rekotiloeng.

### Liprofaele le lekunutu la baithuti

- Sebelisa lebitso la bosoasoi; u se ke ua ngola lebitso le felletseng, nomoro ea mohala kapa lintlha tse ling tse ka tsebahatsang moithuti.
- Ha u eketsa moithuti, khetha setšoantšo, sehlopha le khetho ea bong, ebe u tiisa hore motsoali kapa mohlokomeli o lumetse.
- Sebelisa **Fetola moithuti** ha baithuti ba fetang a le mong ba sebelisa fono.
- Tsoelopele le lintlha tsa profaele li lula fonong eo. Ha li romelloe kapa ho bolokoa seva.

### Litlhophiso le phihlello

Sebelisa **Litlhophiso** ho fetola puo le likhetho tsa ponahalo kapa ho mamela tse fumanehang. Ho bala ka lentsoe ho itšetlehile ka mantsoe a kentsoeng fonong.

### Lintho tse e-s'o phethoe

Sesebelisoa sena ha se e-s'o be le Thuso ea moqoqo e felletseng, lipapali le lisebelisoa tse sebelisanang tse neng li reriloe, litlhahlobo tsa qalong le tsa qetello, pokello ea maikutlo a baithuti, kapa lisebelisoa tsohle tsa matichere le M&E tse sirelelitsoeng ka PIN. APK e kentsoeng fonong ha e e-s'o hlahlojoe ha fono e se na marang-rang.
